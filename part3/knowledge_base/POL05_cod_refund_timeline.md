# Cash on Delivery Refund Timeline

A refund for a cash-on-delivery order is credited to the customer's bank account within 7 to 10 business days after the returned item passes quality inspection. Cash-on-delivery refunds take longer than prepaid refunds because the customer must first supply and verify bank account details, as there is no original payment instrument to reverse the money to. The 7 to 10 business day cash-on-delivery clock starts at quality-check completion, not at the moment the courier collects the item.
