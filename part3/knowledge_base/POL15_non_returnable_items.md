# Non-Returnable Items

Certain categories cannot be returned at all once delivered, including innerwear, socks, cosmetics and personal grooming products, for hygiene reasons. Perishable groceries and made-to-order or personalised items are also non-returnable because they cannot be resold. A non-returnable item is still eligible for a refund or replacement if it arrives damaged, defective or is the wrong product, since those are seller errors rather than a change of mind.
