# Prepaid Refund Timeline

A refund for a prepaid order is returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item passes quality inspection. Refunds to a prepaid card or net banking account are routed back through the original payment instrument, and refunds to a UPI account are usually the fastest to appear. A prepaid refund credited to the Flipkart wallet is processed instantly once quality inspection is complete.
