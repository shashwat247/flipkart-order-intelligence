# Order Cancellation

An order can be cancelled free of charge at any point before it is dispatched, and the cancellation takes effect immediately. An order that has already been dispatched cannot be cancelled directly and must instead be refused at the door or returned after delivery. A cancelled prepaid order is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days, and a cancelled cash-on-delivery order needs no refund because no money was collected.
