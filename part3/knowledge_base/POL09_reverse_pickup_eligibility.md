# Reverse Pickup Eligibility

Reverse pickup means a courier collects the returned item from the customer's address at no charge, and it is available on most apparel, footwear and electronics orders at serviceable pincodes. Reverse pickup is not available at pincodes marked non-serviceable for collection, and in those cases the customer must self-ship the item and claim the courier charge as a reimbursement. A reverse pickup attempt is made up to three times, after which the return request is closed automatically.
