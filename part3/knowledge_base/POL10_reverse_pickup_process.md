# Reverse Pickup Process and Timeline

A reverse pickup is scheduled within 2 business days of the return request being approved. The customer must hand the item over in its original packaging along with any invoice or accessory that came with it, and the courier will not collect an item that fails this check at the door. The returned item reaches the quality-inspection facility within 3 to 5 business days of collection, and the refund clock starts only after that inspection passes.
